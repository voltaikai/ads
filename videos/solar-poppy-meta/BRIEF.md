## Intent

A Meta (Facebook + Instagram Reels/Feed) ad for California Virtual Power Plant Program, a local
solar business in Bakersfield. The owner talks straight to camera. Job: get homeowners to book a
free consultation. The feel is premium, calm and confident, "like an Apple or Linear product
video, not a loud infomercial", and unmistakably on-brand with the Solar Poppy mark.

## Assets

- source/aroll-original.mp4 — owner's talking-head recording (49.17s, 1080x1920, 30fps). Its audio is the only voice track.
- source/solar-poppy-logo.png — the Solar Poppy mark (1440x1440 PNG); rebuilt as assets/solar-poppy-mark.svg.
- assets/aroll-trimmed.mp4 + assets/voice.wav — the trimmed A-roll (48.17s) and processed voice.
- transcript.json — word timings on the trimmed edit, script spelling.

## Customizations

- Trim dead air / breaths > ~0.25s (three pauses shortened to ~0.22s).
- Rebuild the logo as a layered SVG; bloom animation on the end card.
- Synthesized SFX only (sfx/): airy whoosh, soft tick, rising tone, power-on chime. No music.
- Word-by-word subtitles (Montserrat 700), on for the whole video.
- Style test: render the first 8 seconds and wait for approval before the full build.
- 9:16 only (user confirmed; no second aspect ratio).
- Storyboard approved by the user.

## Notes

- Exact palette only: #13304A, #F7941D, #E2711D, #FFD27A, #FFFFFF, #D5DEE7. No pure black.
- Fonts: Sora 400/600/800, Montserrat 700 (subtitles only), Bricolage Grotesque 700–800 (numerals).
- Safe zone: no text/graphics in the top 250px or bottom 650px of the 1920 frame.
- Never on screen: "No debt", "lower bills", invented numbers/stats/prices, Sunrun or PG&E logos, testimonials.
- Compliance footer for the last 5 seconds (text fixed by the user, see STORYBOARD.md Frame 8).
- No stock footage; every cutaway is a motion graphic.
